Generative AI applications need more than a prompt. They also need ways to observe model interactions, connect tools, manage state, and clean up after each operation.

In this learning section, I will explore Gen AI concepts, discover the tools available, and learn how to incorporate them into application. I will update existing chapters and add new ones day by day as I continue learning.
